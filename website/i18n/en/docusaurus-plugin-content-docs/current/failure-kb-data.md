---
title: Failure KB data
slug: /failures/data
---

The initial corpus is restricted to source pages, issues, releases, forum posts, or articles published or observed in 2026. Every canonical record keeps at least one source URL.

A record's `date` is the 2026 source publication or observation date identified by `date_kind`. It must not be interpreted as proof that the underlying defect first appeared in 2026.

Root cause, solution, and workaround fields are factual source-backed fields. When the cited source does not establish a value, the canonical value is `unknown`.
